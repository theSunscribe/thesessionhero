Session Hero Account Deletion

Users may delete their Session Hero account directly within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Session Hero.
  2. Select Profile.
  3. Open Data & Support.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Data Removal

When an account is deleted, the user's Session Hero account and associated personal data are permanently removed from active systems.

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backup recovery purposes.
