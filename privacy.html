Session Hero Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 5, 2026

Session Hero is operated by Sunscribe LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how Session Hero collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Session Hero may collect information you provide or generate while using the app, including:

  • Account information, such as email address and display name
  • Authentication information through email/password, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In
  • Party membership and scheduling information
  • Availability, blackout dates, sessions, announcements, messages, RSVPs, Snack Board entries, and optional birthday information
  • Device, diagnostic, crash, and usage information used to improve app stability and performance

How We Use Information

We use information to provide core Session Hero features, including account access, party coordination, scheduling, messaging, calendar functionality, app performance monitoring, customer support, and product improvement.

Third-Party Services

Session Hero may use third-party services such as Firebase, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, and RevenueCat to support authentication, data storage, crash reporting, analytics, and subscription management.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as needed to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or use trusted service providers that help deliver Session Hero.

User Content

Information you add to parties, messages, schedules, announcements, and related app areas may be visible to other members of the same party or group within Session Hero.

Data Retention

We retain information as needed to provide Session Hero, maintain records, improve the app, and comply with legal obligations. You may request account deletion by contacting us.

Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Session Hero account and associated data, contact us at admin@thesessionhero.com.

Children

Session Hero is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
